5. Placement of the hot rank <= n+1 code (CLI binaries)
   The non-generic fast-engine functions on this path (refresh_active, prepare,
   Calls::prepare_out, Basis::from_single/complete, reference_vectors_ok,
   ReplayNumerator/ReplayMasters methods) are emitted from the single `ninjars`
   codegen unit. They span 0x3312e60..0x3335030 (136 KiB) in A and
   0x345cb10..0x34d3700 (475 KiB) in B: the new rank n+2 modules
   (engine_x2, bases_x2, subtractions_x2, subtractions_series_x2) are placed
   between them. The extra stalls are front-end (op cache) and load-side,
   with no extra instructions, consistent with this wider and shifted hot
   footprint for the smallest case (about 460 ns per call). Not affected:
   width 32 of the same case (1.007 in the full A/B, 1.018 in the reruns), its
   total (live path) at width 1 (1.003 full, 0.98-0.99 reruns), and every
   other key (next highest 1.040, syn-n2-r1 width 1 reduction, 1.018 rerun).
   Not tried: reordering functions at link time (`-Wl,--sort-section=name`
   did not move the sections) or moving the rank n+2 modules out of the
   `ninjars` codegen unit.
